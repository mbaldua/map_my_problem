Place PWA icon files here:
  - icon-192x192.png   (192×192 px)
  - icon-512x512.png   (512×512 px)

Both icons should use the "maskable" purpose so they work correctly with
Android adaptive icons. You can generate them from your logo using:
  https://maskable.app/editor

The manifest.json references both files. Without them the PWA install
prompt may fail or show a broken icon.
